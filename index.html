Checklist

High-signal areas to review

Duplicate or near-duplicate charges

  • Same vendor, amount, and date charged more than once.
  • Invoice number reused across separate payment entries.
  • Card payment and bank transfer both recorded for the same invoice.

Unused credits and credit memos

  • Credit notes issued but not matched against later invoices.
  • Prepaid balances left after account closure or downgrade.
  • Service-level credits promised after incidents but never applied.

Renewal and cancellation mismatch

  • Renewal occurred after a documented cancellation request.
  • Annual plan renewed while internal owner had left or project ended.
  • Auto-renewal price differs from the signed quote or public order form.

Scope, seats, and downgrade gaps

  • Paid seats exceed active users for several billing periods.
  • Downgrade or feature removal did not reduce the invoice.
  • Tax, shipping, or processing fees duplicated across revised invoices.

Process

A lightweight review workflow

  1. Pick a bounded period, such as the last quarter or last renewal window.
  2. Group vendor records by supplier, account owner, invoice number, amount, and payment date.
  3. Flag only issues with documentary support; avoid assumptions about entitlement.
  4. Verify contract terms and official vendor billing rules before making a request.
  5. Send concise, factual messages with invoice references and requested correction.
